How the Portal Works

A simple 3-step digital process designed for citizen accessibility and accountability.

1

Choose Service or Complaint

Select the required service category, complete the structured application form, or describe your grievance with location details.

2

Receive Secure Reference ID

Upon successful submission and database acknowledgment, receive a unique identifier (e.g., MSP-APP-2026-XXXX or MSP-CMP-2026-XXXX).

3

Track Review & Resolution
